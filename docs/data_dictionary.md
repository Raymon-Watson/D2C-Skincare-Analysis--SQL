# Data Dictionary

## Original dataset

|Column|Type|Description|
|-|-|-|
|Category|Text| Category of sold product |
|City|Text| City of order placement |
|Country|Text| Country of superstore location |
|Customer.ID|Text| Unique customer identifier |
|Customer.Name|Text| Name of customer who placed order|
|Discount|Decimal| Discount applied |
|Market|Text| Market location of superstore |
|Unknown Data|Integer| Unknown & unspecified |
|Order.Date|Date & Time| Date of order placement |
|Order.ID|Text| Unique identifier of order |
|Order.Priority|Text| Order priority (High/Medium/Low)|
|Product.ID|Text| Unique product ID |
|Product.Name|Text| Product name |
|Profit|Decimal| Profit from order |
|Quantity|Integer| Number of the product ordered |
|Region|Text| Region of order placement |
|Row.ID|Integer| Unique row ID |
|Sales|Integer| Total sales amount of order |
|Segment|Integer| Customer segment (e.g. consumer/corporate/home office) |
|Ship.Date|Date & Time| Date of order shipment |
|Ship.Mode|Text|Shipping method |
|State|Text|State or region within country |
|Sub.Category|Text| sub-category of product within main category |
|Year|Integer (Date:Year)| Year of order placement |
|Market2|Text| Market region |
|weeknum|Integer| Week number of order placement |
